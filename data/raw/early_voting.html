EARLY & ABSENTEE IN-PERSON VOTING LOCATIONS

2026 General Election

Locations designated as Early Voting have voting tablets. All other voting locations do not.

Absentee in person voting requires voters to complete an absentee in person oath and affidavit envelope which will be reviewed by the division and a bi-partisan review board to determine voter eligibility.

Voters who vote at an Early Voting location have their eligibility determined at the time they vote by verifying their residence address that appears on their voter registration record is current. If it is not, they are required to complete an absentee in person oath and affidavit envelope which will be reviewed by the division and a bi-partisan review board to determine voter eligibility.

Information is subject to change. (Page last updated: October 30, 2026 at 11:51 am)
